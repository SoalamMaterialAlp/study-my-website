Computer Network

Practical No. 3

Aim: To show various types of networking cables and connectors and identify them clearly.

🖥️ Requirements

Different networking cables, connectors, networking components and a computer or laptop.

📌 Purpose

To identify common networking cables and connectors used in computer networks.

Introduction

Networking cables provide a physical medium through which data can travel between network devices.

Common cables include twisted-pair cables, coaxial cables and fiber-optic cables. Different connectors are used with different cable types.

Procedure

01

Collect Cables

Collect different types of networking cables available in the laboratory.

02

Identify Twisted Pair Cable

Observe UTP/STP cables and identify their construction.

03

Identify Coaxial Cable

Observe the coaxial cable and its connector arrangement.

04

Identify Fiber Optic Cable

Observe the fiber-optic cable and identify its connector type.

05

Identify Connectors

Identify commonly used connectors such as RJ-45 and other available connectors.

06

Record Observations

Record the cable type, connector type and its common networking use.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Handle cables and connectors carefully.
  • Do not bend fiber-optic cables excessively.
  • Identify connectors without forcing them into ports.

✅ Result

Thus, various networking cables and connectors were successfully observed and identified.
