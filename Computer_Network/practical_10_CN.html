Computer Network

Practical No. 10

Aim: To study the specifications of various networking devices from different companies available on the Internet and find out their differences.

🖥️ Requirements

Personal Computer or Laptop, Internet connection and web browser.

📌 Purpose

To compare specifications and features of networking devices from different manufacturers.

Introduction

Networking devices are manufactured by different companies and are available in different models and specifications.

Their specifications may include number of ports, supported network standards, transmission speed, wireless standards, interfaces, management features and other technical characteristics.

Procedure

01

Open Web Browser

Open a web browser and connect to the Internet.

02

Select Networking Devices

Select devices such as switches, routers, wireless access points or network interface devices for comparison.

03

Search Manufacturer Specifications

Visit manufacturer websites and observe the technical specifications of selected networking devices.

04

Record Specifications

Record important specifications such as ports, speed, standards, interfaces and supported features.

05

Compare Devices

Compare the specifications of devices from different companies and note their differences.

06

Prepare Observation

Prepare a comparison of the selected networking devices based on their technical specifications.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Use official manufacturer websites whenever possible.
  • Check the exact model number before comparing specifications.
  • Do not treat different models of the same device as identical.

✅ Result

Thus, the specifications of various networking devices from different companies were studied and their differences were identified.
