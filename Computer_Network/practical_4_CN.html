Practical No. 4

Aim: To study the specifications of cables and connectors of various companies and identify their differences using information available on the Internet.

🖥️ Requirements

Personal Computer or Laptop, Internet connection and web browser.

📌 Purpose

To compare the specifications and features of networking cables and connectors manufactured by different companies.

Introduction

Networking cables and connectors are available from different manufacturers. Their specifications may differ in terms of cable category, bandwidth, construction, shielding, material and supported transmission characteristics.

Procedure

01

Open Web Browser

Open a web browser on the computer and connect to the Internet.

02

Search Cable Manufacturers

Search for networking cable specifications from different manufacturers.

03

Collect Specifications

Record cable category, construction, shielding, length and other available specifications.

04

Search Connectors

Observe specifications of connectors offered by different companies.

05

Compare

Compare the collected specifications and note the major differences.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Use manufacturer or reliable technical websites for specifications.
  • Do not assume that cables with similar appearance have identical specifications.
  • Record technical specifications accurately.

✅ Result

Thus, the specifications of networking cables and connectors from different companies were studied and their differences were identified.
