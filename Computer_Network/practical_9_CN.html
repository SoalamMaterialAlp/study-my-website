Computer Network

Practical No. 9

Aim: To show and identify various networking devices such as NIC, Hub, Bridge, Switch, Router, Gateway and Wi-Fi Access Point.

🖥️ Requirements

Networking devices, computer or laptop, network cables and networking laboratory equipment.

📌 Purpose

To understand the physical appearance and basic functions of different networking devices.

Introduction

Networking devices are hardware components used to connect computers, manage network traffic and provide communication between different network segments.

Examples include NICs, hubs, bridges, switches, routers, gateways and wireless access points.

Procedure

01

Observe NIC

Identify the Network Interface Card used to connect a device to a network.

02

Observe Hub

Identify the hub and study its basic function of connecting multiple devices.

03

Observe Bridge

Identify a bridge and study its role in connecting network segments.

04

Observe Switch

Identify a network switch and observe its ports and basic operation.

05

Observe Router

Identify a router and study its role in connecting different networks.

06

Observe Gateway

Identify a gateway device or gateway function used for communication between networks.

07

Observe Wi-Fi Access Point

Identify a wireless access point and observe its antennas and network ports.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Handle networking devices carefully.
  • Do not disconnect active network equipment without permission.
  • Identify devices according to their labels and specifications.

✅ Result

Thus, various networking devices were successfully demonstrated, observed and identified.
