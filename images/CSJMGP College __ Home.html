About

The Institute

CSJM Polytechnic College is the one of the best polytechnic institute in UP. It was situated in AmbedkarNagar district on Faizabad road and started in 2005 by BTEUP, with 30 students in CS branch and 30 student in IT branch. Currently Institute has running four courses of diploma namely Electrical Engg, Electronics Engg, Computer science and Engg and Information Technology. College has its own campus and a huge playground. College have Labs where students can do practicals and gain practical knowledge. We provide games facilities to the students. The institute is committed to creating a disciplined, inclusive and student-focused learning environment where classroom knowledge is strengthened through laboratory work, workshops, seminars, industrial exposure and project-based learning. Our experienced faculty members guide students in developing strong technical fundamentals, communication skills, teamwork and professional confidence. Along with academic progress, students are encouraged to participate in sports, cultural programmes and technical activities that support their overall personality development. The institute continuously works to improve its learning resources and prepare diploma students for employment, higher education, entrepreneurship and responsible service to society.

Principal

Principal's Message

Principal

Shri R.K. Saroj

CSJM Polytechnic College is one of the leading polytechnic institutes in Uttar Pradesh. It is situated in Ambedkar Nagar district on Faizabad Road and was established in 2005 under the Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh. The institute currently offers diploma courses in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, and Information Technology. The college has its own spacious campus, a large playground, well-equipped laboratories, a rich library, and facilities that help students develop practical knowledge along with academic understanding. We also encourage games, cultural activities, technical events, seminars, industrial visits, and community participation for the overall development of every student. Being the Principal of this esteemed college brings both honour and responsibility. Our vision is to educate, inspire, and empower Indian youth with technical skills, professional values, confidence, and social awareness. We continuously work to provide an environment that supports academic excellence as well as strong moral and spiritual character. Our qualified and dedicated faculty members teach, counsel, mentor, and monitor students throughout their learning journey. I believe our students will have a fruitful and meaningful experience on campus, enrich college life with their gifts and talents, and contribute their knowledge and abilities to industry, society, and the nation.
