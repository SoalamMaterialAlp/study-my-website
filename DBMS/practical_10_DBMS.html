Database Management System

Practical No. 10

Aim: To design and perform database operations on a Student Grades Database.

🖥️ Requirements

Personal Computer or Laptop
MySQL/XAMPP
phpMyAdmin

📌 Purpose

To study retrieval, average calculation, update and deletion operations on student grade records.

Scenario

A school database stores student grades using the following fields:

  • StudentID
  • FirstName
  • LastName
  • Subject
  • Grade

Tasks

1. Retrieve Students with Grade A in Math

Retrieve the names of all students with an "A" grade in the subject "Math". The Subject and Grade fields are used to find students who received grade A in Math.

2. Find Average Grade in English

Find the average grade of all students in the "English" subject. The grades of students in English are considered to calculate the average grade.

3. Update History Grade of StudentID 103

Update the grade for StudentID 103 in "History" to "B+".

4. Delete Failed Science Records

Delete all records for students who have failed in "Science" with Grade "F".

Procedure

01

Create Student Grades Database

Create the Student Grades database.

02

Create Required Table

Create the required table for student grades.

03

Define Fields

Define StudentID, FirstName, LastName, Subject and Grade fields.

04

Insert Records

Insert student grade records into the database.

05

Retrieve Math Grade A Students

Retrieve students with grade A in Math.

06

Calculate English Average

Calculate the average grade for students in English.

07

Update History Grade

Update StudentID 103's History grade to B+.

08

Delete Failed Science Records

Delete records having Science as the subject and F as the grade.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Verify StudentID before updating records.
  • Check the subject and grade before deleting records.
  • Use the correct subject while retrieving and calculating grades.
