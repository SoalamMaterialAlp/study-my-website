Practical No. 9

Aim: To design and perform database operations for a Bookstore Inventory System.

🖥️ Requirements

Personal Computer or Laptop
MySQL/XAMPP
phpMyAdmin

📌 Purpose

To study retrieval, update and deletion operations on bookstore inventory records.

Scenario

A bookstore stores information about books using the following fields:

  • BookID
  • Title
  • Author
  • Price
  • Genre
  • StockCount

Tasks

1. List Science Fiction Books

List all books in the "Science Fiction" genre. The Genre field is used to retrieve the required books.

2. Find Books Priced Greater Than 200

Find all books whose price is greater than 200. The Price field is used for this operation.

3. Increase Stock for John Doe

Increase the stock count by 5 for all books authored by "John Doe".

4. Remove BookID 201

Remove the book from inventory having BookID 201.

06

07

Update Stock

Increase stock count by 5 for books authored by John Doe.

08

Delete Book

Delete BookID 201 from the inventory.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Verify the BookID before deleting a book.
  • Check the author name before updating stock.
  • Verify the price and genre conditions while retrieving records.
